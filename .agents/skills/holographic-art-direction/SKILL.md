description: Direct holographic composition, lighting and materials when designing or reviewing the visual identity of New 3D Holographic OS.
---

Goal: a cinematic holographic station that preserves readable controls and real depth.

Activation: when deciding composition, palette, hierarchy or visual states; consult docs/ART_DIRECTION.md before changing the approved language.

Allowed tools: reading local references, Three.js/CSS, the exposed and authorized image generator, view_image and Playwright with WebKit. Chrome requires explicit permission in the current task.

Procedure:

1. Define focus on the core, asymmetric orbits and secondary panels. Keep black #080C14, cyan #66E8FF, violet #B18AFF and white #E6F7FF.
2. Distinguish STANDBY, ANALYSIS and OVERDRIVE by motion, intensity and structure in addition to color. Reserve glow for active signals; use highly legible text.
3. Compose desktop and mobile separately, preserving volume and a functional interface. Build reduced motion into the design.
4. Capture each mode and a real transition; review silhouette, occlusion, contrast and hierarchy. Prioritize three observable defects.

Deliverables: decisions in ART_DIRECTION.md, applied parameters and evidence with viewport/state.

Verification: a real 3D scene, readable and operable controls, mobile composition without overlaps and consistency across modes.

Restrictions: an AI image is a reference, not an implementation or user approval. Do not invent generated assets, do not fake telemetry and do not approve based on a single capture.
